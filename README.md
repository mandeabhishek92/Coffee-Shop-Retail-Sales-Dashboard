# Coffee-Shop-Retail-Sales-Dashboard
A retail operations dashboard analyzing daily revenue, order volume, and peak store traffic across store locations and product lines.

Key Metrics & Visuals:

KPI Scorecards & Sparklines: Total Sales, Total Orders, and Total Quantity Sold paired with daily trend sparklines.

Temporal & Traffic Analysis: Interactive Month-Year slicer, dynamic calendar heatmap, Weekday vs. Weekend sales split, and Hourly × Day of Week sales matrix to pinpoint peak operating hours.

Product & Store Performance: Sales breakdowns by Store Location, Product Category, and Product Type, enhanced with custom report-page tooltips.
